# Full AI Chat hard-codes no behaviour: every behavioural "if" is a Router question Jev answers

**Status: proposed** (2026-11-13, `plans/full-ai-chat.md`; owner decisions D32–D34 and D43 binding, `.scratch/full-ai-chat/DECISIONS.md`, local; nothing built yet).

Full AI Chat's design passed through complaint and decline stamps (HARD 72 h, SOFT 24 h), a no-sell window over his last 4 fan messages (D26), regex pre-checks (`is_content_dispute`, `classify_decline`), an 8-message router window, offer caps (2 a day, 2 open, a runaway breaker), a cap of 4 Free items per Fan, a $30 ceiling on a Free item and a "bought once before" precondition for one. The owner dropped every one of them (D9, D14, D31, D32): each behavioural "if" is a Router question that Jev answers on that Turn, and the Route table is a pure first-match map of those answers with no rule of its own. Code keeps only plumbing and the Send-correctness rules: at most one Offer per Turn, an Unconfirmed offer blocks the next, nothing sent twice; the bought-only dedupe (D13) stays code, in the Sell executor's pool. We chose this because each dropped rule was a guess with no production data behind it, and every Decision row keeps the answers and their `question_version`, so tuning can come from what Full AI Chat actually did. A reader will find no cap on Free items, no sell block after a Make-it-right (an Offer may follow on the very next Turn), no sell block on a decline (it routes to tease, D43), no per-Fan offer limit, no goodbye rule and no sext window (sext follows his last message, D33): these are deliberate, not missing. With no stored state to keep valid, Jev is asked again at every Evaluation point and no answer is cached (R5 P3, D28); a failed request makes the Turn chat at Heat 1 with no Offer, never a silent no-send (an outage would mute every Fan). Going back means re-adding state the Decision ledger was built without (stamp columns, counters, their merge and lift rules).

## Considered Options

- **Stamps or a no-sell window after a complaint** (ticket 04 r2, D26). Rejected (D31, D32): a sell block set without data, needing stamp columns plus merge and lift rules.
- **Regex pre-checks before Jev.** Rejected (D32): a second judge beside Jev, with nothing measured behind it.
- **Caps on Offers or Free items**, or a minimum purchase history before a Free item. Rejected (D9, D14, D32); the Free items per Fan per day are watched after deploy, not capped.
- **Reusing Jev's answers while the Anchor, last outbound, last purchase and config are unchanged** (ticket 06 r2). Rejected (R5 P3): keeping the cache valid costs more than asking again, at about $0.00008 a call.
