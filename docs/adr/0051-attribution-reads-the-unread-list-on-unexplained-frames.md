# Per-Fan read state comes from one shared Unread list read per Unexplained frame, gapped per Creator

**Status: proposed** (2026-11-22, `docs/spec/creator-badges/goal.txt` Phase C, D3, D9–D14, D17–D21, D24 (D15/D16 are Phase F's) in `.scratch/creator-badges/evidence/fable-r3-final.md`; owner constraints binding; nothing built yet).

The Unread counter frame says that some chat changed read state, not which, and the Amber circle and Fan List's unread Edge markers need to know which Fan. On AE a chat stays unread a median 64 s, and ~11 frames per Creator-hour (busiest 36, peak hour 71) have no DM or echo to explain them, against a baseline of 205 OF calls per Creator-hour whose 429s all come from Focus deep walks (measured). We decided the Relay alone attributes: every Unread counter frame, a same-value one included, triggers one Attribution read (`GET /chats?filter=unread`, 1 call per 10 chats, at most 2 pages) diffed against the held chats window, unless it is an Explained frame; reads are single-flight per Creator, at least 5 s apart (no hourly cap, owner amendment 2026-11-23), honour the 429 exit hold, and are made only for Open-tab Creators that are no Dead session and have a proxy (others show a Grey circle); a Fan flips to read only from a Complete Unread list; each Attribution read also refreshes R, the unread chats the one exclusion predicate `excluded_from_badges` removes (owner amendment #3). One read serves every Employee and tab, and hover and pick reads share one Hover cooldown per Creator. With the socket down the Relay polls the Unread list every 15 s for Open-tab Creators. The Badge reads knob (`full` 5 s / `lean` 15 s / `off`) sets the gap or turns all of it off. Why: the Unread list is exactly the counter's set, never marks read and leaves out Muted chats (measured 3/3), so the diff identifies changes in Unread list membership, and gating on unexplained frames keeps the added cost near +12% (~25 OF calls per Creator-hour gross, inferred) flat in Employees. A Fan missing from a Complete list may have been read or muted. Amber uses the last known mute state, so an external mute can count as awaiting reply until a page-0 read confirms it. The cost: amber and per-Fan dots lag a read made elsewhere by the gap (~3–8 s typical, inferred); a missed explanation costs one read, a wrong one hides a change until the next frame or hover; and the Amber circle stays a 25-chat window fold, whose stale-fold race Phase F's event overlay (echo or DM newer than the window's ask) closes.
Owner amendment 2026-11-22: 5 s gap confirmed for `full`; `lean` = 15 s; `off` = no reads (Badge reads knob, default `full`). Restricted chats stay excluded because Employees can't reply to them in our app; restricted-but-replyable is a future idea only.
ASSUMPTION: "creators visible in an open tab" (D18, D20) means Open-tab Creator, `events.open_tab_accounts()` (rung 1: `.scratch/creator-badges/evidence/fable-r2.md:40`, `service/events.py:236`), not Viewing demand; overturned if the spec gates on View lease.

## Considered Options

- **Read on every frame, no explained rule (six hats).** Rejected by the panel's final round: most frames on a CRM-heavy stack are our own, and the rule is a 0-call shortcut that falls back to a read when wrong.
- **Re-read the chats window per frame.** Rejected: 3 calls against 1, and the window fold cannot see past 25 chats.
- **Per-tab or per-Employee reads.** Rejected: cost grows with chatters and each tab's truth ages on its own (ADR-0042's reason).
- **A second lean switch or a per-user flag.** Rejected by the owner: one per-stack Badge reads knob only; lean is one of its values (`full|lean|off`).
