# A Creator is polled fast only under a view lease or functional demand, never because someone is online

**Status: proposed** (2026-11-08, fetch-diet plan, owner decisions binding; `service/transaction_ingest.py` `_fast_poll_account_ids`, `service/events.py`, `service/of_pacing.py`).

The 30 s purchases poll and the money rail, notification bell and roster strip fetches decide "is anyone looking" from presence: any admin online makes every Creator fast (added after a Creator with no `user_accounts` row was never ingested, `transaction_ingest.py` `_online_account_ids`), a chatter online makes the owner's whole roster fast, and the "All" view subscribes every allowed Creator, so on AE the gate saves almost nothing (measured, `.scratch/fetch-diet/audit/r1-measured.md`). We decided Demand is Viewing demand OR Functional demand and nothing else. Viewing demand is a View lease: the app reports the Creators actually on screen (selected Creator, visible money rail and roster strip rows, open chat), renewed every 20 s, expiring after 60 s, unioned across tabs and Employees; the "All" view and permission "all" never lease every Creator by themselves. Functional demand (Sellers, fan replies, welcomes, scheduled sends, list enrollment, ingestion, WS recovery) never slows, overnight included, and no fan write is capped, delayed or dropped to fund a reduction. Only an Idle Creator gets the slow cadence. To keep never-opened Creators current, opening Inflowwww on any page gives every Creator the Employee can access one App-open warm (staggered per proxy), and selecting a Creator refreshes it at once. Why: presence says who is signed in, not what is on screen, and the cut must fall only where nobody waits; the starvation that motivated admin→all is a ledger concern, and the 15-min supervisor, the ledger's only writer, never consulted this gate, so removing it starves nothing. The cost: a lease is a new app→Relay contract every screen that shows Creator data must keep, and a Creator on a screen that forgets to lease falls to the slow tier.

## Considered Options

- **Keep presence (SSE subs, `of_pacing.last_user_at`) as the signal.** Rejected: SSE subs cover every Creator in the "All" view and stay on 10 min after the last action; they are inputs a lease may reuse, not the lease.
- **Leave admin-online and chatter→owner-roster fast.** Rejected: one signed-in admin made every Creator fast all day; the data an admin needs arrives through the App-open warm and their own leases.
- **Poll everything slow and rely on the WS pump.** Rejected: the pump catches about no unlock events, so a Seller would re-offer content the fan already bought.
