# Full AI Chat's Decision ledger is append-only, one row per Turn, and Router state is a fold over it

**Status: proposed** (2026-11-13, `plans/full-ai-chat.md`; ledger ticket 08 r2 with R6 changes, router-state ticket 07 r3, sell-executor ticket 10 r3; migration after head `0116_scrape_history_deferred`; nothing built yet).

Full AI Chat has to keep every routed Turn for tuning and to own the Offer→purchase join (63 % of the old fastt PPVs had no link to a purchase, so their revenue could not be attributed). We decided on one table, `ai_decisions`: one Decision row per Turn and Mode, made idempotent by a non-null `trigger_key` (the Anchor's id, `wake:<parent_id>` or `test:<session>:<n>`), UNIQUE per (account, fan, mode, trigger_key). The Offer lives on its row (price, media, item ref, and `offer_message_id`, UNIQUE where not null), so there is no offers table and no child table for bundles: one PPV message is one Offer even with four media. Every row records the Jev answers, `question_version` and the Router model, plus its Writer once it writes a reply (none in Shadow), and has no stamp columns. Rows are kept for good; their bulky JSON is nulled at 90 days only once terminal, and test rows go after 7 days. A Turn writes only its own row and never updates another's, so the one piece of Router state, the Pending tease, is a pure fold over the Fan's rows plus the outbound messages and purchases the Turn reads anyway, with no state table, fans column, `state_version` or lease token. The purchase join records its provenance (`join_src`: notice, tx_linked, is_paid, heuristic, ambiguous), because the price-and-time linker is not exact proof (`service/transaction_ingest.py`). We chose this because one writer per row removes the update races a mutable per-Fan slot would bring, the UNIQUE key gives each Turn one runner (no fencing token in v1), and the same fold serves replay, shadow, test and live. It is kept a separate table (ADR-0036).

## Considered Options

- **A mutable per-Fan slot (an `ai_enrolments` column or a fans column) with `state_version` and lease tokens for the Pending tease** (ticket 07 review). Rejected: derived rows have no writer race, and the UNIQUE `trigger_key` already claims the Turn.
- **A child offer table for bundles** (ticket 08 A8). Rejected (ticket 10 r3): one PPV is one Offer.
- **Complaint and decline stamp columns.** Dropped with the stamps themselves (ADR-0032).
