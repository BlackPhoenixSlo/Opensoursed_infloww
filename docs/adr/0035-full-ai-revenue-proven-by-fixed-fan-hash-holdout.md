# Full AI Chat's revenue is proven against a fixed fan-hash holdout of about 20 %

**Status: proposed** (2026-11-13, `plans/full-ai-chat.md` goal; owner D11, ledger ticket 08 r2, rollout ticket 12 r3; nothing built yet).

Shadow mode cannot observe a purchase and replay cannot prove revenue (Fable R1), so the only proof Full AI Chat has is live. We decided that every Fan on a Full AI Chat Creator gets an Enrolment at his first eligible inbound in live mode with selling on (Shadow and the selling-off stage enrol no one, R8; until then the Arm hash still decides who gets Full AI Chat), in both Arms: about 80 % treatment and about 20 % holdout by a salted hash of the Fan, with `salt_ver` and `pct` on the row, fixed for good. A Holdout fan keeps today's Lanes exactly as they are, their own selling included. The win measure is Revenue per enrolled fan (14 days, every source, net of refunds and chargebacks), treatment against holdout, and Full AI Chat widens by adding Creators, never by moving the percentage. We chose this because a split inside one Creator holds her content, her mass sends and her chatters constant and counts sales the treatment takes from them, while writing rows for both Arms keeps the cohort rebuildable (this overturns ticket 08's draft "the holdout writes nothing"). One fixed split means one Arm per Fan and a cohort that never shifts. The cost: about a fifth of an enabled Creator's Fans never get Full AI Chat while the measure runs; the power figures are only a lower bound (about 480 Fans to read +100 %, about 12,000 for +20 %); and the holdout compares the Lane with today's Lanes, not router quality, which is read from replay ablation and per-Route buy rates (Fable R4). Re-salting or changing the percentage reshuffles Arms and breaks every comparison in flight, so any change needs a new `salt_ver`. The 50/50 Writer split (D30) also hashes the Fan but is not part of this decision: it is a model choice recorded on every Decision row that writes a reply (Shadow rows have none).

## Considered Options

- **A percentage knob, such as a 5 % first sell slice** (ticket 12 A-R2). Rejected: two moving cohorts, and no owner ask for it; it can be added later.
- **Replay on the gold set as revenue proof.** Rejected: replay measures routing, never a purchase.
- **Enrolling only treatment Fans.** Rejected (ticket 08 r2): the holdout cohort could not be rebuilt.
