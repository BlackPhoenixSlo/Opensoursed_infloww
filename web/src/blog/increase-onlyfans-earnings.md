OnlyFans income grows through a handful of levers: subscriptions, PPV, tips, live streams and referrals. Each depends on the same groundwork: knowing your audience, making content worth paying for, replying well, and promoting consistently. Pull one lever at a time and measure it.

## The levers

- **Subscriptions:** steady base income. Grow it with promotion and retention, not just price.
- **PPV in messages and posts:** paid extras for fans who want more. Often a large share of income.
- **Tips:** voluntary, and driven by good conversations and recognition.
- **Live streams:** events that give regulars a reason to show up and tip.
- **Referrals:** OnlyFans runs a referral programme for bringing new creators to the platform. Check its current terms in your account.

The fee applies to fan payments: subscriptions, PPV, tips and streams. OnlyFans keeps 20% of each payment before VAT, per its terms at the time of writing; check the current figure in its Help Center. Referral earnings follow the referral programme's own terms. Price with that in mind.

## Use your own data

Guessing what fans want is slow. Your data is faster:

1. List your top 20 spenders and what they bought.
2. Rank last month's PPV items by revenue, not by unlocks.
3. Note the hours when fans reply and buy.
4. Check which promotion channel brought each new paying fan.

Then make one change based on what you found, and check the effect next month.

## Content worth paying for

- **Be specific.** Fans should know what your page offers and get it every week.
- **Mix formats:** feed posts, PPV, behind-the-scenes, streams.
- **Describe paid items honestly.** Fans who get what they expected buy again.
- **Raise quality where it shows:** lighting and sound often matter more than a new camera.

## Engagement that pays

**Send a welcome message.** Every new subscriber should hear from you soon after joining: a thank-you, what to expect, and an easy question to start a conversation.

**Stay engaged.**
- Reply personally and promptly, within set hours.
- Remember details and refer back to them.
- Thank every tip and purchase by name.
- Check in with fans who have gone quiet, without pressure.

**Be consistent.** A predictable schedule builds trust, and trust renews subscriptions.

## Grow the audience

- **Promote on one or two channels** that suit your content, within their rules.
- **Collaborate** with creators in a neighbouring niche.
- **Run time-limited promos** and free trials for specific campaigns.
- **Track every channel** with its own link, and drop what doesn't convert.

## A monthly routine

1. Review revenue by lever and by channel.
2. Pick one lever to improve.
3. Make one change, such as a new PPV tier, a welcome message rewrite or a new channel.
4. Measure it for a month.
5. Keep it or roll it back.
