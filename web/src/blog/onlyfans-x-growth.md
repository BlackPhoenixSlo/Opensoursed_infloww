Short answer: grow on X in stages, and don't skip ahead. First a profile that converts, then consistent posting, then genuine engagement, then partnerships with other creators. Paid promotion only makes sense once those four work, because before that you are paying to send people to a page that leaks.

X allows adult content when it is marked as sensitive, and it allows direct links to OnlyFans. Read X's rules on adult content and sensitive media, and set your media settings accordingly, before you start.

## Stage 1: a profile that converts

Every post you make sends people to your profile. Make it ready for them.

- **Username:** the same as your OnlyFans name if possible. If it is taken, add or drop a letter rather than adding numbers or underscores.
- **Display name:** your name plus a short niche keyword, so you show up in X search.
- **Profile photo:** sharp, well lit, the same one you use elsewhere. Your face if you show it; a consistent brand image if you don't.
- **Header:** recent and on-brand. Check it on both phone and desktop, where it crops differently.
- **Bio:** who you are, what you post, and a call to action. "New sets every Friday, I reply to every message. Link below."
- **Link:** a tracking link for X, not your bare profile URL, so you know what X sends you.
- **Pinned post:** your best recent post with your link. Replace it when something beats it.

## Stage 2: post on a rhythm

Consistency is what makes X show you to people. Decide on a daily number you can keep for a month and keep it.

- Mix **teasers** (cropped or covered previews), **personality posts** (your day, opinions, humour) and **direct promotion** (offer plus link).
- Post media more than text. Images and short videos travel further.
- Use a handful of **hashtags** that match the post: a couple of broad ones and a couple of niche ones. Research what creators in your niche use.
- Save your best posts and repost them weeks later with a new caption. Most followers never saw them.

Our post on building an X posting schedule has a full daily template.

## Stage 3: engage like a person

Broadcasting gets ignored. Conversation gets reach.

- **Reply to every comment** on your posts for the first hour.
- **Spend 15 minutes after each post** replying thoughtfully to creators and accounts in your niche.
- **Build X lists:** one for creators in your niche, one for repost and promotion accounts, one for your most engaged fans. Check each list daily and engage with what you see.
- **Join X Communities** for creators and read their rules before posting.
- **Answer DMs** politely and briefly, and move serious conversations to OnlyFans.

Avoid mass-following and unfollowing, repetitive replies, and automated engagement. They look like spam to users and to X.

## Stage 4: grow with other creators

Once you post consistently, partnerships multiply your reach.

- **Repost trades:** agree with creators your size to repost each other on set days.
- **Shout-out swaps (S4S):** a post recommending each other's pages, timed for when both audiences are active.
- **Repost accounts:** some large accounts repost creators in a niche. Ask before tagging, and if they charge, treat it as advertising and measure it.
- **Collaborations:** joint content needs every participant verified with OnlyFans first.

Give each partner their own tracking link. If you run Inflowwww, that is **Tracking links** (`/growth/tracking-links`), and you can see which partner sends subscribers, not just likes. ## Then, and only then, paid promotion

When your profile converts and you know your numbers, you can test paid promotion: paid repost accounts or ad placements that accept adult creators. Start small, use a dedicated tracking link per test, and stop anything that doesn't pay for itself within a few weeks.

Review X analytics weekly: impressions, profile visits, link clicks. Put them next to subscribers per tracking link, and let that decide what you do next week.
