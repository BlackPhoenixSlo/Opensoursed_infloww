A watermark will not stop every leak, but it makes your work traceable. When a photo turns up on a site it should not be on, a visible mark connects it to you, helps the takedown request, and points some viewers back to your page. Here is how to design one, where to put it, and how to apply it in bulk.

## Why it matters

- **Proof of ownership.** A visible name or handle makes it obvious whose work it is.
- **Takedowns.** Copyright takedown notices (such as DMCA notices in the US) are easier to file when the content is clearly yours. Many creators also use professional takedown services.
- **Traceability.** If you add a fan-specific mark to customs or high-value PPV, you can tell where a leak came from.
- **Free promotion.** A leaked photo with your handle on it still sends some people to your page.

Watermarking is one layer. Keep your originals, keep upload dates, and report leaks through the platform's and the hosting site's official channels.

## What a watermark is

A watermark is a mark laid over the image or video: usually your handle, a logo, or both. It can be:

- **Visible:** text or a logo you can see. Deters casual reposting.
- **Semi-transparent:** visible but light, so it does not ruin the image.
- **Unique per buyer:** a small code, username or number on custom content.

Most creators use one semi-transparent handle on everything and a unique mark on customs.

## Designing your watermark

- **Use your handle, not your legal name.** The point is to identify the page, not you.
- **One clean font,** readable at small sizes.
- **White or black at 30-50% opacity,** whichever contrasts with your usual backgrounds. Keep one of each.
- **Keep it small** but not tiny: roughly 5-10% of the image width.
- **Save it as a PNG with a transparent background** so you can drop it onto anything.

## Where to place it

- **Not on the edge.** Corner marks are cropped out in seconds.
- **Over the subject,** near the middle of the frame, where cropping it out would ruin the image.
- **Vary the position slightly** between photos so a single crop template does not work for a whole set.
- **On video,** keep it on screen the whole time, and consider moving it every few seconds.

The harder it is to remove without damaging the image, the better it works.

## How to apply it

- **On the platform.** OnlyFans has offered a built-in watermark option in its settings that adds your handle to uploads. Check your current settings for what it does and where it is placed.
- **Phone editors.** Many built-in editors and apps (Snapseed, Canva) can add a text or image layer.
- **Desktop.** GIMP (free) or Photoshop for photos; batch actions apply the same mark to a whole folder.
- **Online tools.** Free batch watermarkers exist; check what they do with your files before uploading anything private.
- **Video.** DaVinci Resolve (free tier), CapCut, Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro can overlay a PNG for the full length.

Batch it. Watermark a whole set in one pass right after editing, before anything goes in your vault.

## A simple workflow

1. Edit the set.
2. Batch-apply the standard watermark.
3. For customs and high-value PPV, add a small unique code and note which fan received it.
4. Upload to your vault and file by set and date.
5. Keep unwatermarked originals offline, backed up.
