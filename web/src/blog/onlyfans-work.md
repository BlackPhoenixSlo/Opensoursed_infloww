OnlyFans is a subscription platform. Creators charge for access to a private feed, earn more through paid messages, tips and custom content, and get paid out to a verified account after the platform takes its fee. Here's each part of the system, and what to set up first.

## Getting started: verification

Before you can earn anything, OnlyFans verifies that you are an adult and who you are. You'll provide identification and set up your payout details. Anyone who appears in your content with you also has to be verified and complete the platform's release process.

Before you set up, decide:

- **Your stage name** and a dedicated email address for the account.
- **Your niche**: adult content, fitness, cooking, cosplay, music or something else.
- **Your limits**: what you will and won't create.

Then read the Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy. They define what you can post.

## How creators earn

There are several income streams, and most successful accounts use more than one.

1. **Subscriptions.** Fans pay a monthly fee to see your feed. You can also run a free page and earn through other streams instead. Check the help centre for current price limits.
2. **Bundles and promotions.** Discounts for longer subscriptions or limited-time offers help keep fans subscribed.
3. **Pay-per-view (PPV) messages and posts.** Content sent or posted with a price; fans pay to unlock it.
4. **Tips.** Fans can tip on posts, messages and streams.
5. **Custom content.** Personal requests, priced by you, within your limits.
6. **Live streams.** Free or paid, with tips during the stream.

## How messaging works

Messages are often the biggest earner and the biggest time commitment. You can chat one-to-one, send a message to many subscribers at once, attach paid content, and set up a welcome message for new subscribers.

Good practice:

- Welcome every new subscriber.
- Set reply hours, and keep to them.
- Keep notes on regulars: what they bought, what they like, what they declined.
- Never move payments off the platform.

If an agency or assistant chats for you, give them your limits in writing and agree how account access works.

## How payment works

- **The fee.** OnlyFans keeps 20% of each payment before VAT, per its terms at the time of writing; check the current figure in its Help Center. Plan your prices from the 80% you keep.
- **Payouts.** Earnings become available after a holding period and are paid to your verified bank account or another supported method. Payout schedules, methods and minimum amounts are in your account's payout settings; check them there, because they change.
- **Chargebacks.** If a fan disputes a payment with their bank, OnlyFans' [terms](https://onlyfans.com/terms) say the amount is deducted from your earnings. Describe paid content accurately to reduce disputes.

## How tax works (in general)

In most countries, creator income counts as self-employment income, and nobody withholds tax for you.

- **Record every payout and every business expense** from day one: equipment, props, software, part of your phone or internet bill where that's allowed.
- **Set money aside** each time you're paid.
- **Read your tax authority's guidance**: the IRS (irs.gov) in the US, HMRC (gov.uk) in the UK, the CRA (canada.ca) in Canada. US creators may receive a tax form from OnlyFans; the IRS explains what it means.
- **Consider an accountant** who works with creators.

Rules and thresholds vary by country and year. This is not tax advice. Check the current rules with your country's tax authority or an accountant.

## How growth works

OnlyFans has limited built-in discovery, so most growth comes from promotion elsewhere: social platforms, communities and collaborations, each with its own rules. Post on a consistent schedule, use tracking links to see which channels bring subscribers who stay, and keep engaging with the fans you have. Retention is cheaper than acquisition.

**Safety basics.**

Use a unique password and two-factor authentication, watermark your media, consider geoblocking your local area, and keep your stage identity separate from your personal one. Geoblocking is a filter, not a wall: anyone using a VPN can get past it. Block, restrict and report anyone who crosses a line.
