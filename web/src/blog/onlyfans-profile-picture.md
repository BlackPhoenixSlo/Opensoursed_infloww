Your profile picture has one job: make a visitor stop long enough to read your bio. It is shown small, next to your name, in search results, comments, messages and shared links, so it has to work at thumbnail size.

## Match the picture to your persona

Before you shoot, decide what the picture should say about you in one glance: playful, glamorous, sporty, cosy, artistic. Then choose a photo where that comes through in expression, outfit, colour and setting.

- Use the same picture, or one from the same shoot, on your other platforms. Fans arriving from social media should recognise you instantly.
- Keep it consistent with your banner. The two sit side by side and should look like one brand.
- Choose a photo that will still feel right in six months. Changing it often makes you harder to recognise.

## How much to show

The profile picture is public. Anyone can see it, including people who arrive from a shared link, so it should stay within what the platform allows in public areas.

- Show your style and personality, not your best paid content. The picture should create curiosity, not answer it.
- A clear expression and eye contact, if you show your face, tend to make people stop.
- If you do not show your face, choose a signature element instead: a hairstyle, an outfit colour, a pose, a prop. Make it recognisable and use it consistently.
- Avoid group photos. Visitors should know immediately which person the page belongs to.

## Technical basics

Small images punish small mistakes.

- **Square crop with a centred subject.** The picture is shown as a circle or square, so anything near the edges can be cut off.
- **High resolution.** Upload a large, sharp image; the platform scales it down. Never enlarge a small photo.
- **Good light.** Soft daylight from a window or a ring light in front of you. Avoid harsh overhead light and strong backlight.
- **Simple background.** A plain or blurred background keeps attention on you.
- **Contrast.** Make sure you stand out from the background at thumbnail size.
- **Test it small.** Shrink the photo to the size of a fingernail on your screen. If you cannot tell what it is, pick another.

## Test and refresh

Once a picture is live, give it a few weeks and watch how many profile visitors subscribe. If you change the picture, change nothing else for the next few weeks so you can see whether it made a difference.

Refresh the photo when your look changes significantly, so fans who meet you in messages or on the feed see the same person they saw in the thumbnail.

## A quick checklist

- [ ] Fits your persona and matches your banner and social profiles.
- [ ] Within the platform's rules for public images.
- [ ] Square, centred, sharp, well lit, simple background.
- [ ] Recognisable at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Kept for long enough to measure.
