Most creators earn modest amounts and a small group earns a great deal, so "how much can you make" has no single answer. What you can control is the mix: subscriptions bring fans in, and PPV messages, tips and custom content are where most growing accounts earn the bulk of their money.

## The income streams, one by one

**Subscriptions.** Fans pay monthly for access to your feed. You set the price within the range OnlyFans allows (check the current minimum and maximum in your settings). You can also run a free page, a paid page, or both.

**PPV messages.** Paid content sent in chat or mass messages. Fans unlock what interests them, which suits fans who don't want a higher subscription.

**Tips.** Voluntary payments on posts, in chat or during streams. For many accounts they are a small share of income, but they grow with good conversations.

**Custom content.** Made to order for one fan. Usually the highest price per item and the most time per item.

**Paid posts and streams.** Locked posts on the feed and paid live sessions.

OnlyFans takes a platform fee from earnings before payout. Check the current percentage in its terms and plan with your share, not the gross.

## Why the average won't tell you much

OnlyFans does not publish full earnings data, so most quoted figures come from third-party estimates. Even good estimates are averages over millions of accounts, many inactive. They are pulled up by a few very large accounts and down by dormant ones.

Better questions:

- What did your median month look like over the last six?
- What share came from each stream?
- What was revenue per paying fan?

Those answers tell you where the next increase can come from.

## Pricing each stream

- **Subscription:** price for your niche and test one change at a time for a few weeks. Raising price usually lowers new subscribers; watch total revenue, not headcount.
- **PPV:** keep a small ladder of price points. Describe what the fan gets honestly; disappointed buyers stop buying.
- **Customs:** publish a clear menu with a starting price, turnaround time and what you don't do. Ask for payment before you start.
- **Tips:** thank every tip personally. It takes a minute and helps keep regulars around.

## Grow the base: promotion and consistency

1. **Pick one or two promotion channels** that suit your content and follow their rules on linking.
2. **Use a separate tracking link per channel** so you can see which one brings paying fans.
3. **Post on a schedule** fans can predict.
4. **Reply to messages promptly.** Engaged fans renew and buy more.
5. **Review monthly** and drop what doesn't convert.
