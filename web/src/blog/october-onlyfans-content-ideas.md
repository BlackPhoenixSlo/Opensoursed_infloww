October is a month with a finale. Everything can build toward Halloween, which means you can plan the month backward from one night. Here are thirty-one ideas, a handful of real dates to hang them on, and a plan to shoot it all early.

## The dates worth using

- **October 1:** International Coffee Day. A morning post, mug in hand.
- **First Friday:** World Smile Day. A cheerful, low-effort post.
- **October 4:** World Animal Day. Pets on camera, if you have one.
- **October 16:** World Food Day. Cook something on camera.
- **October 31:** Halloween.

Fill the rest with weekly themes. A recurring day ("every Tuesday is a tease day") is easier for fans to remember than a new idea every time.

## Week 1 (1-7): ease in

1. **Autumn wardrobe.** First knitwear of the season.
2. **Midweek check-in.** A selfie and one question for the DMs.
3. **Coffee morning.** Mug, robe, window light.
4. **Smile day.** A laughing, candid set.
5. **Pet cameo.** If you have one, it gets the post.
6. **Sundress.** One last summery look before it gets cold.
7. **Slow Monday.** Soft light, quiet audio, relaxed content.

## Week 2 (8-14): personality and polls

8. **Teaser Tuesday.** Crop of a set you will release as PPV later.
9. **Pizza night.** Order in, film a casual evening.
10. **Niche day.** Lean into your page's specific angle.
11. **Costume poll.** Three options for your Halloween set.
12. **Weekend set.** A full photo set from your main shoot.
13. **Comfort day.** Loungewear, no makeup, real life.
14. **Bold look.** A style that is the opposite of your usual.

## Week 3 (15-21): build toward the night

15. **Reading nook.** A rainy day, a blanket, your book.
16. **World Food Day.** Cook or bake on camera.
17. **Outfit of the day.** Fans vote on tomorrow's.
18. **Leggings day.** Gym or casual, styled.
19. **Water day.** A bath or shower-themed set, styled and tasteful.
20. **Behind the scenes.** Prepping the Halloween costume.
21. **Pumpkin day.** Carve one with a fan-chosen design.

## Week 4 and the finale (22-31)

22. **Snack ranking.** Rate five autumn snacks on camera.
23. **Watch-along.** Announce a horror-movie live.
24. **Costume reveal.** The poll winner, first photos.
25. **Celebration drink.** Sparkling wine or a mocktail, dressed up.
26. **Live stream.** The movie or a game night.
27. **Scenic Sunday.** Autumn leaves, outdoor light, somewhere private.
28. **Throwback.** Last year's Halloween next to this year's.
29. **Role swap.** A persona you would not normally play.
30. **Halloween eve.** Final teaser for the bundle.
31. **Halloween.** Full costume set, a live, and the themed bundle.

Planned once, posted all month.

## A backward plan from October 31

| By | Do |
|---|---|
| September 25 | Pick three costumes; buy props |
| October 1 | Shoot weeks 1-2 in one session |
| October 11 | Costume poll closes |
| October 15 | Shoot weeks 3-4 and the costume set |
| October 24 | Reveal; announce the Halloween bundle and live |
| October 31 | Release the bundle; go live |
