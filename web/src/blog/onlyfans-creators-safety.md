For most creators, the risks that matter are ones you can reduce yourself: personal details leaking through content, account takeover, payment fraud and chargebacks, and the strain of managing fan relationships. Each one has a practical countermeasure. Here they are, in order of how much harm they prevent.

## Protect your identity

Your real name and location are the most valuable things to protect, because once they leak you can't take them back.

- **Use a stage name** everywhere: profile, promotion accounts, and any signature on messages.
- **Separate your accounts.** Create a dedicated email address and, where you can, a separate phone number for creator work. Keep personal social profiles private and never link them to your creator accounts.
- **Check every frame** before posting: mail, packages, street views from windows, landmarks, school or work logos, and distinctive tattoos you would rather keep private.
- **Strip metadata.** Turn off location tagging in your phone camera, and check files before upload.
- **Use geoblocking.** OnlyFans lets you block viewers by location. Many creators block their own area to reduce the chance of people they know finding the page. Geoblocking is a filter, not a wall: anyone using a VPN can get past it.
- **Turn on watermarking** so your username is on your media if it is reposted.
- **Run a reverse image search** on your profile and promo photos now and then, to spot reposts and fake accounts using your pictures.
- **Think about free previews.** Anything posted publicly can be copied. Keep your most sensitive content for paying subscribers.

## Lock down your account

Account takeover is one of the most damaging and most preventable risks.

1. **Use a unique password** for OnlyFans and for the email account behind it. A password manager makes this easy.
2. **Turn on two-factor authentication** with an authenticator app. Then a stolen password alone is not enough to get in. Save the backup codes offline. A lost phone should not lock you out of the page that pays your rent.
3. **Secure your email.** Your email is the reset key for everything else, so give it its own strong password and 2FA.
4. **Change your password** straight away if you notice a login, setting change or message you don't recognise.
5. **Know who has access.** If an agency or assistant works on your account, agree in writing who has access, how, and how it is removed when the arrangement ends. The same goes for tools: any app, extension or CRM that logs in for you holds the keys to your account. Treat a captured session like your password.

## Watch for phishing

Fraudsters impersonate platforms and brands to steal logins.

- OnlyFans and other legitimate services will not ask for your password by email, message or phone.
- Check sender addresses and links carefully. Look for misspelled domains and unexpected attachments.
- When a message claims to be from the platform, don't click its link. Open the site yourself and check your notifications there.
- Be wary of "brand deals" or "collaborations" that ask you to log in through a link they send.

## Keep your earnings safe

- **Use the platform's built-in payments.** Payments routed outside the platform carry more fraud risk and may break the platform's rules.
- **Understand chargebacks.** OnlyFans states that charged-back amounts are deducted from the creator's earnings. Read the current wording in OnlyFans' [terms](https://onlyfans.com/terms).
- **Reduce disputes** by describing paid content accurately and delivering what you promised.
- **Keep records** of sales, customs and conversations so you can respond if a dispute arises.
- **Secure your bank and payout accounts** with the same strong-password and 2FA habits.

## Protect your wellbeing

Safety includes your mental health.

- **Set working hours** for replies and stick to them. Constant availability wears people down fast.
- **Decide content limits** for the week in advance, so pressure in the moment doesn't decide for you.
- **Filter negativity.** Block, restrict and report abusive fans. You don't need to read every comment.
- **Have a support network**: peers who understand the job, people offline who know you, and a professional if things get heavy.

## If something goes wrong

- **Harassment or threats:** save evidence (screenshots with dates), block the person, report them to OnlyFans, and contact local police if you feel in danger.
- **Leaked content:** use OnlyFans' and the hosting site's takedown processes. Keep your original files as proof of ownership.
- **Account compromise:** reset your email password first, then the platform password, then contact OnlyFans support.
