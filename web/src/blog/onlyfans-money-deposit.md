OnlyFans earnings first sit in a pending balance for a holding period, then become available to withdraw. After you withdraw, the money still has to travel through your payout method and your bank, which adds a few business days. The hold, the minimum withdrawal and the processing time differ by country and payout method, and they change. OnlyFans lists the processing time per method in Banking > Payouts; check it there. Your Statements page shows each earning as pending or available, so you can see the date for yourself.

## The two clocks

Every payout runs on two clocks:

1. **The platform hold.** New earnings show as pending on your Statements page and become withdrawable after the holding period. New accounts, or accounts in some countries, can have a longer hold for an initial period.
2. **The transfer time.** Once you withdraw, OnlyFans processes the request and sends it. Your payout method and bank then take their own time, usually a few business days. Weekends and public holidays add to it.

So a sale made today reaches your bank account after the hold plus the transfer time. Budget with both in mind.

## Manual or automatic withdrawals

- **Manual:** you request a payout when you choose. Good if you want control, or you prefer to withdraw in larger, less frequent amounts.
- **Automatic:** OnlyFans sends your available balance on a schedule you pick. Good for steady cash flow with no reminders.

Check whether your bank or payout method charges per incoming transfer. If it does, fewer, larger withdrawals cost less.

## Before your first payout

Most first-payout delays come from setup. Before you request one:

- **Verify your account** and complete identity checks.
- **Submit tax forms,** such as the W-9 for US creators or the equivalent form for your country.
- **Add payout details in your own name.** OnlyFans requires the account to belong to you.
- **Double-check every digit** of account and routing numbers, or IBAN and SWIFT codes.
- **Confirm your bank accepts the transfer.** Some banks restrict payments connected to adult content; see [our guide to choosing a bank]({{ '/blog/onlyfans-bank-usa/' | url }}).

## Why a payment is late, and what to do

Work through these in order:

1. **Still pending?** Check the Statements page. If the money hasn't cleared the holding period, it isn't late yet.
2. **Refunds or chargebacks?** If a fan's payment was reversed, your balance is adjusted. The Statements page shows it.
3. **Wrong details?** A single wrong digit can bounce a transfer. Check your payout settings, then wait for the returned funds to show again before you retry.
4. **Weekend or holiday?** Add the missing business days.
5. **Bank review?** Banks sometimes hold incoming transfers for checks. Call them and ask whether a transfer is sitting in review.
6. **Still stuck?** Contact OnlyFans support through the help centre in your account, with the payout date, amount and method. Keep a written record of every contact.

If a payout shows as processed but has not arrived, follow [this troubleshooting list]({{ '/blog/onlyfans-payout-not-in-bank/' | url }}).

Creator communities can help you compare notes, but for anything involving your account or money, rely on official support.

## Plan cash flow around the delay

- Keep a buffer of at least one month of essential costs, so a late payout isn't a crisis.
- Move your tax share into a separate account as each payout arrives. This is not tax advice. Check the current rules with your country's tax authority or an accountant.
- If you pay chatters or editors, pay from money that has landed, not from pending balances.
