Thanksgiving sits right before Black Friday, so it is a sales moment, and it is a holiday where heartfelt posts tend to beat themed ones. Here is how to run the sales, what to post, and a five-day plan.

US Thanksgiving falls on the fourth Thursday of November. If many of your fans are elsewhere, adapt: Canadian Thanksgiving is the second Monday of October, and a simple "thank you" week works anywhere.

## Thanksgiving sales

**Bundle deals.** Package your best November sets, or your best of the year, as one PPV at a clear discount. State what is included: number of sets, photos, minutes.

**Early-bird subscriber discounts.** Offer a discounted first month to new subscribers who join in the week before Thanksgiving. Use a free trial link or a promo with a set end date.

**Thanksgiving Day flash sale.** A 24-hour discount on a specific bundle. Announce it in the morning; send a reminder in the evening to fans who have not bought.

**"Leftovers" deal.** On the Friday or weekend after, sell the unused frames and outtakes from your November shoots as a low-priced bundle.

Rules for all four:

- Say the end date and stick to it.
- Do not raise prices the week before to make the discount look bigger.
- Send reminders only to non-buyers.

We would offer a Thanksgiving discount too, but $0 does not go lower.

## Content ideas

**Autumn outdoor shoot.** Leaves, knitwear, warm golden light. Shoot it early in November so you have teasers ready.

**Cooking stream.** Make a side dish or a pie live, in a themed apron outfit, chatting with fans while it bakes.

**"What I'm thankful for" post.** A sincere, personal post: a milestone, a lesson, your community. This is the one that tends to get the most replies. Keep it honest.

**Dinner-themed collab.** Two or more creators, a shared "Friendsgiving" theme, each posting the same day and tagging the others. Agree on the dates and the split in writing first.

**Unboxing special.** A surprise box, a new outfit, a seasonal haul, unboxed on camera.

**Gratitude set.** A themed photo set where each photo carries a short caption thanking fans for something specific from the year.

**Black Friday haul tease.** "What I bought on Black Friday", posted on Friday, with a hint that your own sale is on.

## Five-day plan

| Day | Feed | Messages |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Autumn set teaser | Announce early-bird offer |
| Tuesday | Behind the scenes of the cooking prep | None |
| Wednesday | "What I'm thankful for" post | Teaser for the flash sale |
| Thursday | Cooking live; gratitude set | Flash sale: morning launch, evening reminder |
| Friday | Black Friday haul tease | "Leftovers" bundle; reminder to non-buyers |

## Boosting engagement around the holiday

- **Ask a question.** "What's on your table this year?" is an easy reply.
- **Reply to everyone** who answers the gratitude post, even with a short line.
- **Thank the buyers** personally after the sale.
- **Keep the tone warm.** Fans are often with family; short, kind messages work better than heavy sales pushes.
