How do you launch a creator on mym? Hit four milestones in order: a profile people can find, a content buffer banked before day one, a steady post-launch rhythm, and Push messages that bring warm fans back. Launches rarely fail on talent. They fail because the page runs out of posts in week two. We do not support mym, so the product stays out of this one.

## Milestone 1: build a profile people can find

mym has internal discovery surfaces, which means a well-built profile can be found by people already on the platform. Build for that.

- **Bio:** say what the page offers and how often it posts, in the language of the audience you want.
- **Search terms:** mym has search. Use words in the bio that match what the creator actually offers. Honest keywords convert better than popular ones that disappoint.
- **Location:** if the creator's brand ties to a city or region, add it.
- **Entry offer:** ask the platform's team which subscription options are available for your creators, such as a free tier or a promotional price, and test one against the other.
- **Operations:** before traffic, decide who chats, who posts and who approves content. Write it on one page.

## Milestone 2: bank content before launch

A fan who arrives to an empty feed leaves. A buffer protects the launch from a slow shoot week.

Minimum before launch day:

- Captioned posts for the first two weeks. If you plan two posts a day, that is close to thirty.
- A small paid catalog, five to ten items, so the first wave of visitors has something to buy without starting a conversation.
- A script library for common chat moments: welcome, first offer, follow-up, thank-you.

A content buffer is the cheapest insurance an agency can buy.

## Milestone 3: run the post-launch rhythm

Once the page is live, reach comes from posting and engagement.

- **Cadence:** at least two free posts a day. Push to three in week one to build momentum.
- **Format mix:** test a split of video and photo posts and check engagement weekly per creator.
- **Free share:** keep a meaningful share of posts free and public so the feed has something to show new visitors.
- **Timing:** start with evening hours in the audience's main time zone, then move toward whatever your own numbers show. Each audience is different.
- **Outside traffic:** keep sending it through tracked links. Engagement from outside visitors helps the platform's discovery pick up the creator.

## Milestone 4: bring warm fans back with Push

Push messages drive repeat sales and renewals. Segment every send:

| Segment | Goal | What to send |
|---|---|---|
| Current subscribers | Retention and sales | Exclusive items, short-dated vouchers |
| Former subscribers | Reactivation | A teaser of what they are missing |
| Interested non-buyers | First purchase | A small discount on a catalog item or bundle |

Rules of thumb: always include a teaser, bundle a handful of catalog items together for better value, and use a clear deadline. Check the platform's own frequency rules for each segment and stay well inside them; fans who feel spammed unsubscribe.

## The 30-day rollout

- **Week 0:** profile done, buffer banked, catalog stocked, scripts loaded, chat coverage assigned.
- **Week 1:** three posts a day, first tracked traffic sources live, first Push to current subscribers.
- **Week 2:** compare sources by subscribers per visit. Cut the weakest, double the best.
- **Week 3:** set a weekly loop of Push, chat follow-up and catalog updates. Re-engage lapsed fans.
- **Week 4:** review revenue per subscriber against the creator's other platforms and decide on month two.
