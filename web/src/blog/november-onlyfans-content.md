November has two jobs: keep the momentum from Halloween and build toward the biggest sales week of the year. Here are thirty ideas, anchored on real dates, and a plan for shooting them in a few sessions.

## The dates that matter

- **November 1-2:** Día de los Muertos. A respectful, art-led look works; avoid using it as a costume without context.
- **All month:** Movember, the men's health campaign. Good for male creators and for a charity tie-in.
- **November 13:** World Kindness Day.
- **November 19:** International Men's Day.
- **Fourth Thursday:** US Thanksgiving.
- **The Friday after:** Black Friday. **The Monday after:** Cyber Monday.

Plan the sales around the last week. The rest of the month is there to warm fans up.

## Week 1 (1-7): settle into autumn

1. **Halloween leftovers.** Post the unused frames from October's costume shoot.
2. **Nail day.** A fresh manicure or pedicure, close-up and styled.
3. **Sports day.** Wear your team's colours for a game-day set.
4. **Gear review.** Rate three things you own and use for content.
5. **Cosy set.** Knitwear, blankets, warm light.
6. **AMA.** Collect questions all week; answer on video.
7. **Teaching day.** Share a skill: a recipe, a workout, a makeup look.

## Week 2 (8-14): personality week

8. **Fire and candlelight.** A warm, low-light set.
9. **Superhero day.** A costume you already own, or a fan-voted character.
10. **Slow Sunday.** Unhurried, calm content.
11. **Me-time.** Your real self-care routine.
12. **Happy hour.** A drink recipe, dressed up.
13. **Kindness day.** Free post for everyone, or a thank-you message to long-time fans.
14. **Music day.** Share a playlist and film a short dance.

## Week 3 (15-21): build the list

15. **Niche day.** Lean fully into whatever your page is known for.
16. **Fan-choice outfit.** Ran a poll in week 2? Shoot the winner.
17. **Shy day.** Soft, modest, mostly-covered looks.
18. **Princess day.** Dress-up, glam, crowns.
19. **International Men's Day.** Male creators: a Movember update; everyone: a thank-you to male supporters.
20. **Sneak peek.** First teaser of your Black Friday bundle.
21. **Behind the scenes.** The planning for next week's sale.

## Week 4 and the tail (22-30): the sales week

22. **Favourite things.** A "what I own and love" post, gift-guide style.
23. **Second sneak peek.** A different frame from the bundle.
24. **Sale announcement.** Dates, price and what is included.
25. **Mess day.** Baking, painting, a messy-room-to-tidy-room transformation.
26. **Thanksgiving.** A sincere thank-you post, plus a light themed set.
27. **Black Friday.** Launch the sale.
28. **Reminder.** Message fans who have not bought yet.
29. **Last call.** The final day of the sale.
30. **Recap and preview.** Thank the buyers; tease December.

The sale has an end date. Our price does not have a start date.

## Shooting it in four sessions

- **Session A (late October):** cosy, candle, nail, and slow sets (days 2, 5, 8, 10, 17).
- **Session B:** costumes and glam (9, 18, plus unused October frames).
- **Session C (mid-November):** bundle content and both sneak peeks (20, 23).
- **Session D:** the fan-voted and Thanksgiving content (16, 26).

Talking-to-camera days (AMA, teaching, recap) take ten minutes each. Film them on the day.

### Running the sale week

- Use a list of fans who have **not** bought, so buyers do not get the reminder.
- Write three messages in advance: announcement, reminder, last call.
- Keep the discount honest. If it is 30% off, say 30% off, and do not raise the base price the week before.
