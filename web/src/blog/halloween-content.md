Halloween is one of the easiest content holidays of the year. Fans expect costumes and themes, so a persona change feels natural rather than random, and the whole of October can build toward one night. Here are twelve ideas and a plan to run them.

## Costumes and themed sets

**1. Costume showcase.** Shoot three costumes in one session and let fans vote on which one gets a full set. The vote is engagement; the winning set is PPV.

**2. Trick-or-treat roleplay.** A light, playful scenario: fans "knock", you choose trick or treat. Keep the script short and the tone fun.

**3. Polaroid series.** Instant-camera shots in costume, scanned and posted as a numbered set. Limited numbers make them collectible, and a scan keeps everything on the platform.

**4. Themed photo set with props.** Pumpkins, candles, fake cobwebs, a fog machine. One afternoon of props gives you a week of posts.

## Games and interactive content

**5. Spin the wheel.** Fans tip to spin a wheel of prizes: a voice note, a photo, a costume choice, a discount. Make every slot something you are happy to deliver.

**6. Pumpkin carving.** Carve live or on video, and let fans choose the design. Simple, wholesome, and very October.

**7. "Companion for the night".** A themed run of personal messages through Halloween night, priced as a bundle. Write the scope down before you sell it.

## Livestreams

**8. Horror movie marathon.** Watch a classic together on a live stream, in costume, reacting in real time. Check the film's streaming rights before you show it on screen; often it is safer to react while fans watch on their own screens.

**9. Horror game stream.** Play something scary and let tips trigger in-game challenges.

**10. Halloween recipe live.** Cook something themed: pumpkin bread, a spooky mocktail, decorated cookies.

**11. ASMR storytelling.** A ghost story told in a whisper, with ambient sound. Audio is quick to make and works for creators who prefer not to show their face.

## The Halloween sale

**12. A themed sale.** A "Spooky Season" discount on subscriptions or a bundle of everything you posted in October.

- Announce it a week before.
- Run it for 48-72 hours around October 31.
- Send a reminder on the last day, to non-buyers only.

## A three-week plan

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Oct 10-15 | Shoot costumes and props in one or two sessions; plan the wheel prizes |
| Oct 16-20 | Costume vote; first teaser crops |
| Oct 21-27 | Release sets; pumpkin carving; announce the sale and lives |
| Oct 28-31 | Sale live; movie or game stream; ASMR story on the night |
| Nov 1 | Thank buyers; post a recap; tease November |
