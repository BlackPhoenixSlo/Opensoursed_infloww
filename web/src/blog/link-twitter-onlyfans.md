Short answer: you connect X from your OnlyFans account settings, approve the permission screen on X, and you are done. It is quick, it helps fans trust that your page is really you, and it gives you a second place to promote.

Below: the steps, what the link does and does not do, and how to fix it when it won't connect.

## Why link X at all

Two different things get called "verification" on OnlyFans, and it helps to keep them apart.

- **Identity and age verification** is mandatory for every creator. OnlyFans checks a government ID and a live selfie and confirms you are at least 18. Linking X does not replace it.
- **A linked social account** is a public trust signal. It shows visitors that the person on the OnlyFans page also runs an established X profile, which makes impersonation harder and makes subscribing feel safer.

X is the account most creators link because its rules allow adult content when it is labelled correctly. That makes it the one place where your public profile and your OnlyFans can point at each other openly.

## How to link the accounts

Menu labels move around. If your settings still offer a linked-accounts option, the flow is:

1. Log in to OnlyFans on a desktop browser. It is easier than the mobile site for permission pop-ups.
2. Open your settings and go to the account section.
3. Find the connected or linked accounts option and choose X (it may still say Twitter).
4. You are sent to X. Log in to the account you want to show, read the permission screen and approve it.
5. You land back on OnlyFans with the account shown as connected.

Check it from the outside: open your OnlyFans profile in a private browser window and confirm the X link appears and points to the right handle.

## When the link fails

Work through these in order. Most failures are one of the first three.

- **Wrong X account.** If your browser is logged in to a personal X account, that is the one that gets offered. Log out of X first, then retry and log in with the creator account.
- **Pop-ups or third-party cookies blocked.** Allow them for both sites, or try a different browser.
- **Stale session.** Log out of both sites, clear cookies for them, log back in and retry.
- **X account restrictions.** A locked or newly created X account can fail the authorisation. Resolve it on X's side first.
- **Still stuck.** Contact OnlyFans support from the help section of your account and include the exact error text and a screenshot.

Don't use third-party "verification services" that ask for your passwords. Only OnlyFans and X need to see your login.

## No X account yet

You do not need a large following before you link. A clean, active profile does more for trust than a big dormant one.

- Create a dedicated creator account with the same handle you use on OnlyFans.
- Mark your media as sensitive in X's settings if you post adult content, as X's rules require.
- Pin one post that introduces you and links your page.
- Post consistently for a couple of weeks before you lean on it for promotion.

If X is not for you, OnlyFans offers other ways to show a public presence, such as linking another social profile or a personal site. Check the options in your settings.

## For agencies with several creators

Each creator should own their own X account, with their own email and two-factor authentication. The agency gets access the way the creator chooses, and the creator can always revoke it.

Keep one simple record per creator: X handle, date linked, who has access, and when 2FA was last checked. When a link breaks, that record is the first thing you look at.
