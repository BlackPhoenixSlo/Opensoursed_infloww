No tool earns money on its own; the team and the content do. What software can do is put the right fan, the right file and the right words in front of a chatter at the right time. Here are five workflows that do that in Inflowwww, and one cost line it removes outright.

## 1. Start more conversations

{% shot "messages-pro.webp", "Messages Pro with a fan chat list and an open thread with an image and a PPV", "Messages Pro with chats across every creator. Demo data." %}

Inflowwww does not message fans automatically when they come online. Conversations start with a person:

1. Open **Messages Pro** and check **Last seen** in the fan insights rail. Fans who were just on are the best people to message.
2. Keep three or four openers in **Growth > Scripts**, and edit each one with something from the fan's **Notes** before sending.
3. Spend the first 15 minutes of every shift on recently seen fans, before working the unread queue.

A specific opener ("Did you get to the concert?") gets more replies than a generic one, every time.

## 2. Send mass messages to the right segment

**Growth > Smart lists** builds saved audiences from rules over spend, recency, status and tags. Combine rules with **ALL** or **ANY**, check the live count, and save. Then in **Growth > Smart Messages**, send to a list and exclude others.

Three segments worth building on day one:

- **Active, never spent**: a gentle free preview.
- **Past buyers**: first look at a new set, priced.
- **Top spenders**: excluded from broadcasts entirely, so they only get personal messages.

Send one mass message per segment per week at most, and schedule it for the hour your fans buy (the Message dashboard export shows when).

## 3. Win back expiring and expired fans

Automatic expiry messages are not built; the trigger half of Smart Messages is not part of the product. What you can do today:

1. Build a Smart list of expired fans, with a spend rule if you only want past buyers.
2. Turn on **Growth > Auto-follow** for recently expired fans with a small daily cap. A follow-back is a notification, not a message.
3. Once a week, send the expired list a short win-back offer from **Smart Messages**.

Then watch **Rebill** and **New subs** in Creator reports for that creator.

## 4. Sell PPV with context

{% shot "fan-reports.webp", "Fan reports listing the top spenders", "Fan reports: who spends, before you price a PPV. Demo data." %}

Before offering anything, a chatter should glance at two things:

- **Recent PPV purchases** in the fan insights rail, so you never offer what they already own, and you can see the prices they have paid before.
- **Lifetime spend** and **Tips**, to set a price that fits this fan rather than a fixed menu.

Then attach the file from the vault in the composer. If the owner has labelled the vault in **Growth > Vault Pro** with tags, descriptions and captions, this takes seconds instead of minutes.

## 5. Write replies faster, without an AI copilot

We do not ship an AI reply assistant. The older AI features in the code are off by default and not part of the product. What helps instead is a good Scripts library:

- Save every message that sold, the same day, as a template.
- Name templates so they sort into small sequences (`intro-1`, `intro-2`, `offer`).
- Review the library weekly and delete what has gone stale.

A team that shares its best lines writes faster and sounds more like the creator than one starting from a blank box.
