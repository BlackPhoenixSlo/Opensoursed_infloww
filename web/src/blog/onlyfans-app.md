No. At the time of writing there is no official OnlyFans app in Apple's App Store or the Google Play Store. You use OnlyFans in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

## Why there is no app

Apple's and Google's app store policies restrict apps built around adult content. OnlyFans hosts adult content, so it runs as a website instead. The mobile site has the same core features as desktop: the feed, messages, the vault, statistics and settings.

## How to put OnlyFans on your home screen

This gives you a one-tap icon without installing anything.

**iPhone (Safari)**

1. Open the OnlyFans website in Safari and log in.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Choose "Add to Home Screen" and confirm.

**Android (Chrome)**

1. Open the OnlyFans website in Chrome and log in.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app" and confirm.

Menu names vary slightly by browser version, but the option is in the share or main menu in every major mobile browser.

## Be careful with look-alike apps

App stores and websites sometimes list apps that use the OnlyFans name or logo, or claim to be a mobile client. Treat them with caution.

- They are not made by OnlyFans.
- Many ask for your username and password. Anyone holding your login holds your earnings, messages and content.
- Some exist only to collect logins or show ads.

To stay safe:

- Use the official website, typed in yourself or from a saved bookmark.
- Turn on two-factor authentication in your security settings.
- Use a password manager and a unique password for the account.
- Check your active sessions in settings now and then, and log out any you do not recognise.

## What OFTV is

OFTV is a separate service from the company behind OnlyFans. It hosts free, safe-for-work video, such as fitness, cooking, music and lifestyle content, and it is available as an app because it contains no adult content.

OFTV is not a mobile version of OnlyFans. Your OnlyFans subscriptions, messages and purchases do not appear there. Some creators use it as one more place to show their personality and their non-adult skills.

## Working from a phone

Many creators run most of their page from a phone. A few habits make that easier:

- Keep your creator work in a separate browser profile, or a separate browser, from your personal browsing.
- Batch editing and captions on a laptop if you can, and use the phone for posting and messages.
- Save drafts and schedule posts ahead, so you are not tied to the screen.
- Back up your camera roll before you clean it.
