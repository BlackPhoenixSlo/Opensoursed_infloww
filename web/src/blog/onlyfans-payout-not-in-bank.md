"Processed" means OnlyFans has released the money, not that your bank has received it. The usual reason for the gap is ordinary transfer time: a few business days, longer for international transfers and around weekends and holidays. If it goes beyond that, the cause is usually wrong account details, a bank review, or a bank that restricts this kind of income.

## What "processed" actually means

A payout moves through three stages:

1. **Pending:** earnings are in the holding period and can't be withdrawn yet.
2. **Processed:** OnlyFans has sent the payout to your payout method.
3. **Received:** your bank or wallet has credited it to your account.

Between stages 2 and 3, the money is with the payment network and your bank. OnlyFans can confirm it was sent; only your bank can confirm it arrived.

## First, give it the normal window

Before you investigate, check the calendar:

- **Domestic bank transfers** usually take a few business days.
- **International transfers** take longer, and may pass through one or more intermediary banks.
- **Weekends and public holidays** don't count as business days, in your country or the sender's.
- **E-wallets** may credit faster, but moving money from the wallet to your bank is a separate transfer with its own timing.

If you're still inside that window, wait. If you're past it, work through the list below.

## The usual causes

**Incorrect account details.** One wrong digit in an account number, routing number, IBAN or SWIFT code can send a transfer back. Returned funds can take days to reappear in your OnlyFans balance. Check your payout settings carefully against a bank statement.

**Name mismatch.** The name on the bank account should match the verified name on your OnlyFans account. Some banks reject transfers when they don't match.

**Bank review.** Banks sometimes hold incoming payments for checks, especially large, international or first-time transfers. The money is often visible to the bank but not to you.

**Bank policy.** Some banks restrict payments connected to adult content and may return or hold the transfer. If this happens, read your account terms and consider a bank whose written policy accepts creator income.

**Technical problems.** Occasionally a payout is delayed by an issue at OnlyFans, the payment network or the bank. These usually resolve without action, but keep records in case they don't.

## Troubleshooting, step by step

1. **Screenshot the payout** on your Statements page: date, amount, method and status.
2. **Check your payout details** in settings for typos and name mismatches.
3. **Call your bank.** Give them the date, amount and sender, and ask whether a transfer is pending, under review or returned. Ask for any reference number.
4. **For international transfers,** ask whether an intermediary bank is holding it, and whether fees were deducted on the way.
5. **Contact OnlyFans support** through the help centre in your account. Include the payout details and what your bank told you. Ask for the transfer reference if they can provide one.
6. **Keep a log** of every contact: date, who you spoke to, and what they said.

Don't share login details, codes or documents with anyone offering to "speed up" a payout. Use official channels only.

## Prevent the next delay

- Keep payout details current, and update them before closing any bank account.
- Withdraw early in the week to avoid weekend gaps.
- Keep a buffer of essential costs, so one late payout isn't a crisis.
- If your bank has held transfers before, consider a second account at a different institution.
