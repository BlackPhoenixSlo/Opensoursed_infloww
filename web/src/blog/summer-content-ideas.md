Summer gives you the best free light of the year and a dozen ready-made themes. Here are twenty-nine ideas grouped by setting, most of which need nothing you do not already own.

## Outdoors and backyard (1-7)

1. **Golden-hour set.** The hour before sunset, on a balcony or in a private garden.
2. **Picnic.** A blanket, fruit, a book. Easy, bright, summery.
3. **Garden work.** Watering plants, a sun hat, muddy gloves.
4. **Hammock or sun lounger.** Relaxed, lazy, warm.
5. **Sprinkler or hose.** Playful and very summer.
6. **Backyard barbecue.** Cooking outdoors, an apron, a cold drink.
7. **Hiking or nature walk.** Views, trail outfit, a short vlog.

Outdoors means public or semi-public. Shoot on private property or far from other people, follow local law, and check every frame for anything that shows your location: house numbers, street signs, landmarks.

## Water: pool, beach and bikini (8-13)

8. **Poolside lounging.** Sunglasses, a float, a drink with an umbrella in it.
9. **Swimwear try-on.** Three or four looks; fans vote on the favourite.
10. **Underwater shots.** An action camera or a waterproof phone pouch.
11. **Beach day vlog.** Packing the bag, the drive, the sea.
12. **Wet hair, after the swim.** A natural, low-effort set.
13. **Sunscreen routine.** Practical, and a reminder to wear it.

## Staying cool indoors (14-18)

14. **Air-conditioned afternoon.** Lounging, a fan, loose summer clothes.
15. **Cold shower or bath.** Styled and tasteful.
16. **Ice and frozen treats.** Ice lollies, iced coffee, a cocktail on ice.
17. **Ice cream taste test.** Rank five flavours on camera.
18. **Summer reading.** A stack of books and your verdicts.

## Active and sweaty (19-23)

19. **Outdoor workout.** Early morning, before the heat.
20. **Run or cycle vlog.**
21. **Beach volleyball, paddleboarding, tennis.** Whatever you actually play.
22. **Post-workout.** Sweaty, honest, a little tired.
23. **Summer fitness challenge.** Seven days, fans join in.

Heat is a real risk. Shoot active content early or late, drink water, take breaks in the shade, and stop if you feel dizzy. No set is worth heatstroke.

## Fruit, food and summer nights (24-29)

24. **Fruit platter.** Watermelon, peaches, berries. Colourful and bright.
25. **Smoothie or cocktail recipe.**
26. **Summer night set.** Fairy lights, warm skin, a balcony.
27. **Stargazing live.** A late, quiet stream with music.
28. **Bonfire or candles.** Warm, flickering light (safely, with water nearby).
29. **End-of-summer recap.** The best frames of the season, and a teaser for autumn.

## Getting the most out of summer light

- **Shoot early and late.** Midday sun is harsh and creates hard shadows.
- **Find open shade** for soft, even light on bright days.
- **Use a reflector** (or a white towel) to bounce light into shadows.
- **Protect your gear.** Phones overheat in direct sun; keep them in the shade between takes.
- **Batch outdoor days.** One good-weather afternoon can give you two weeks of posts.

### Plan the season, not the day

1. List the twenty-nine ideas by setting.
2. Pick four shoot days: one backyard, one water, one indoor, one evening.
3. Shoot several ideas per day.
4. Spread the posts across six to eight weeks.
