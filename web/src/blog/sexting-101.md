Paid sexting is a service: an adult, text-based conversation that a fan pays for and a creator runs on their own terms. Done well, it is one of the most personal things a page can offer. This guide covers the professional side of it: limits, consent, pacing, persona and price. It does not contain example messages, because your words should be yours.

## Set your limits before the first session

Write down, before you offer sexting at all:

1. **What is on the menu and what never is.** Topics, roleplay themes, language you will and will not use.
2. **Which formats are included.** Text only, or also photos, voice notes or short clips, and which of those cost extra.
3. **Session length.** A fixed time (for example 20 or 30 minutes) or a fixed number of messages.
4. **Hours.** When you take sessions, and that requests outside them wait.
5. **Hard stops.** Requests that end a session immediately, such as anything involving minors, non-consent, real people who have not agreed, or anything the platform prohibits.

Put the public parts on your page or in a pinned menu, so fans know the rules before they pay. A team may help with messages. OnlyFans' [terms](https://onlyfans.com/terms) allow creators to use assistants, but the account holder stays responsible for everything sent, and fans must not be deceived about who they are talking to in a way that breaks the platform's rules or the law. If chatters run sessions for you, give them the same list to follow exactly. No chatter should ever agree to something you did not approve.

## Consent is ongoing, on both sides

- **Agree the scope at the start.** A short message confirming what the session includes and how long it runs sets the frame.
- **Check in as you go.** A light "still good?" or "want to keep going this way?" keeps things comfortable for both of you.
- **You can stop at any time.** If a fan pushes past a limit, end the session politely and firmly. Payment buys the agreed service, not unlimited access.
- **Respect the fan's limits too.** If they hesitate or change the subject, follow their lead.
- **Never share a fan's messages or details outside the team.** Notes about a fan belong in your fan notes, not in a group chat or a screenshot.

## Pace the session

Good sessions build gradually rather than jumping straight in.

- **Start with conversation.** A few warm, personal messages first help both sides relax. Many fans are nervous at the start.
- **Ask open questions** to learn what the fan enjoys, within your menu.
- **Mirror their pace.** Long, thoughtful messages deserve the same; short ones signal they want things quicker.
- **Use your time to think.** Unlike a call, text gives you a moment to compose each reply. Use it.
- **Change direction if it feels off.** You are allowed to steer the conversation.
- **End warmly.** A friendly close and a thank-you make it more likely the fan books again.

## Stay in persona

Your sexting style should match the persona fans already know from your feed. If your page is playful, sessions should be playful. If it is confident and assertive, the same applies. Consistency is what makes it feel real.

Keep a persona sheet: tone, phrases you use, phrases you avoid, and fixed facts about the persona. It helps you on a tired day and it keeps a chatting team consistent. Approved non-explicit phrasings, such as openers, check-ins and closings, can live in **Scripts** (`/growth/scripts`) if you run Inflowwww; write the rest of each session fresh.

## Price it as a service

- **Price by time or by message count,** and say which in your menu.
- **Charge before the session starts,** usually via a tip or a paid message that confirms the booking.
- **Price extras separately:** custom photos, voice notes or clips.
- **Offer a package** for regulars, such as three sessions at a lower rate.
- **Review your prices** every few months. If you are always booked, they are probably too low.

Keep a simple record of sessions: date, length, price and anything to remember for next time. Your OnlyFans statements already show tips and paid messages. ## Look after yourself

Sexting can be emotionally demanding, even when it goes well. Cap the number of sessions you take per day, take breaks between them, and stop offering the service whenever it stops feeling right.
