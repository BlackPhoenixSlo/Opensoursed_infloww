You need fewer apps than most lists suggest. Cover six jobs, shooting, editing, design, scheduling, analytics and storage, with one tool each, and add business software only when the page becomes a team.

## Shooting

Your phone camera is enough to start. What makes the difference is support around it.

- **A tripod with a phone mount** and a **Bluetooth remote** or the camera's built-in timer, so you can shoot hands-free.
- **A ring light or softbox.** Light improves quality more than any camera upgrade.
- **Burst or interval shooting** in your camera app, to capture many frames per pose and pick the best later.

Before you install a third-party camera app, check what permissions it asks for. A camera app has no need for your contacts.

## Editing and design

- **Photos:** Adobe Lightroom is the common choice for colour and light correction, with presets so a whole set looks consistent. Snapseed is a free alternative on phones.
- **Video:** CapCut handles cuts, captions and transitions on a phone. DaVinci Resolve is a free desktop editor if you want more control.
- **Tip:** create one preset or "look" and apply it to every set. A consistent look reads as a brand.

Read each app's terms on what content it allows you to upload to its cloud features. Some editing apps restrict adult content in their cloud services; working on files stored on your device avoids the question.

**Design**

- **Canva** for banners, promo graphics and text overlays, with free templates.
- **Photopea** for a free, browser-based layer editor.

Keep a simple brand kit: two or three colours, one or two fonts, your logo or handle. Use it on every graphic.

## Social scheduling

Promotion depends on posting consistently on other platforms, and schedulers make that possible without living on your phone.

- **Buffer** and **Later** queue posts across several social networks.
- Each social network also has its own native scheduler, which avoids giving a third party your login.

Check every scheduler's acceptable-use policy and each social network's rules on adult promotion before you connect accounts.

## Analytics

- **OnlyFans statistics** show earnings, subscribers and post engagement. Check them weekly.
- **Each social platform's native analytics** shows which promo posts drive profile visits.
- **Tracking links** per platform tell you which source actually brings subscribers.

A spreadsheet that combines these once a month is often more useful than another app.

## Storage and backup

Content is your inventory. Losing it is expensive.

- Keep every original in at least two places, for example an external drive and a cloud service whose terms allow your content.
- Organise by date and set, with the same folder structure in both places.
- Encrypt drives and turn on two-factor authentication on cloud accounts.

The most reliable copy is the one on Your Own Disk™, backed up somewhere else.
