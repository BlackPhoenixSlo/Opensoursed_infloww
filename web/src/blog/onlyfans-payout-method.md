OnlyFans pays creators through bank transfer and, depending on your country, other methods such as international wires and e-wallets. The right one is the fastest method your bank accepts with the lowest total fees. The available methods, minimum withdrawal and fees vary by country and change over time, so treat your account's payout settings and OnlyFans' help pages as the source of truth.

## How payouts work, in brief

- OnlyFans keeps 20% of each payment before VAT, per its terms at the time of writing; check the current figure in its Help Center. Your balance shows your share.
- New earnings are pending for a holding period before you can withdraw them.
- You withdraw manually or set automatic withdrawals on a schedule.
- The money must go to an account in your own name.

At the time of writing, PayPal is not offered as a payout option. If you see a third party offering to "cash out" OnlyFans earnings through another account, don't use it. Payouts go to your own verified account only.

## The method types, compared

**Domestic bank transfer.** Where it's available, usually the simplest choice: direct to your bank, often with no OnlyFans fee, typically arriving within a few business days. Accurate account details are essential.

**International bank transfer or wire.** For creators outside the main banking regions. Available widely, but usually slower, and fees can come from OnlyFans, intermediary banks and your own bank. Ask your bank what it charges to receive one.

**E-wallets.** Where offered in your country, an e-wallet can be quicker to receive. You then move the money from the wallet to your bank, which can add another fee and another wait. Check the wallet's own fees and limits.

Whichever you choose, compare on three things:

1. **Total cost:** OnlyFans fee, any intermediary fee, your bank's incoming fee and currency conversion.
2. **Time to usable money:** not just to the wallet, but to the account you pay bills from.
3. **Reliability:** does your bank accept the transfer without review holds?

## Setting up and changing your payout method

The exact menu labels change from time to time, but the flow is:

1. Open your account's **banking or payout settings**.
2. Complete identity verification and the tax form for your country. This is not tax advice. Check the current rules with your country's tax authority or an accountant.
3. Add your payout details, in your own name.
4. Wait for OnlyFans to verify them.
5. To switch, choose a new default payout method in the same section. Payouts after the switch go to the new method.

Avoid changing methods in the middle of a pending withdrawal. Let it complete first.

## Requesting a payout

- Check your available (not pending) balance on the Statements page.
- Make sure it is above the minimum withdrawal for your method.
- Request a manual payout, or set an automatic schedule.
- Note the date and amount, so you can trace it if it's late.

## Mistakes that delay withdrawals

- A typo in the account number, IBAN or SWIFT code
- A name on the bank account that doesn't match the verified OnlyFans account
- A bank that restricts payments connected to adult content
- Withdrawing just before a weekend or public holiday
- An unfinished tax form

If a payout is stuck, contact OnlyFans support through the help centre in your account, and ask your bank whether the transfer is under review.
