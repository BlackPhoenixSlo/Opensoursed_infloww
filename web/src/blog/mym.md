Looking at mym for your creators? Start here. mym is a fan platform with its own discovery feeds, Push messages and a paid catalog, and it rewards agencies that run a tight daily routine. Inflowwww does not connect to mym, so these pages are advice only.

## The operating loop in one paragraph

Finish the profile, bank content, post public content every day, send outside traffic through tracked links, and let engagement pull in the platform's own discovery. Then sell through three channels: chat with new subscribers fast, send segmented Push messages weekly, and keep the paid catalog growing. Review the numbers every week and change one thing at a time.

## What to prepare before the first creator goes live

- **People:** who chats, who posts, who approves, and who reads the weekly numbers. Write names, not roles.
- **Content:** at least two weeks of captioned posts and a small catalog of paid items.
- **Language:** one profile per language. Match every traffic source to the profile's language.
- **Links:** one tracked link per source, all pointing at the creator's profile.
- **Scripts:** a welcome message and three or four tested follow-ups, written in the creator's voice.
- **A stop rule:** the result at day 30 that decides whether you continue.

## How to tell a mym page is working

Read these weekly, per creator:

- Subscribers per visit, per tracked link.
- Revenue split by chat, Push and catalog.
- Subscribers that arrived without one of your links, which is roughly what discovery adds.
- Chat response time to new subscribers.

Then diagnose by pattern. Strong subscribers with weak sales point at chat. Strong engagement with weak conversion points at price or offer. Good posting with flat growth points at traffic. Weak everything usually means the routine slipped.

## Three habits that matter more than any tool

- **Speed on new subscribers.** The first minutes after a subscription decide a lot. Have someone on shift before you send traffic.
- **A weekly Push, segmented.** Current subscribers, former subscribers and interested non-buyers each get a different message with a different goal.
- **A growing catalog.** Add paid items on a steady cadence so new visitors always find something recent.
