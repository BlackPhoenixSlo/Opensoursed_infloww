Fitness pages work because the content is a routine: fans come back to see the next session, the next lift, the next result. Here are twenty-five ideas in four groups, enough to post daily for a month without repeating a format.

## Warm-up content (1-5)

Warm-ups are short, easy to film, and useful to fans who actually train.

1. **Morning mobility.** A five-minute routine, filmed in one take.
2. **Stretch-along.** Real time, with a timer on screen, so fans can follow.
3. **Foam rolling.** Show the spots and explain why.
4. **Activation drills.** Glute bridges, band walks, shoulder circles, with form cues.
5. **Pre-workout ritual.** Playlist, drink, outfit, the moment before the first set.

## Weightlifting content (6-12)

6. **Form check.** Film a lift from the side and narrate the cues.
7. **PR attempts.** Build up across a week, then attempt the lift on camera.
8. **Leg day, full session.** Edited to two minutes, with the full version as PPV.
9. **Upper body split.** Same format, different day.
10. **Gym outfit of the day.** A quick set before the session.
11. **"Train with me".** A point-of-view clip of a full workout.
12. **Progress comparison.** The same lift or pose, a month apart.

Lifting tip that applies to content too: progressive overload. Add one new format a week rather than five at once.

## Cardio content (13-18)

13. **Run vlog.** A morning route, with views and a time.
14. **HIIT circuit.** Twenty minutes, filmed so fans can follow at home.
15. **Jump-rope challenge.** Count reps; challenge fans to beat it.
16. **Cycling or spin.** Indoor or outdoor, with a playlist.
17. **Dance cardio.** A short choreography fans can learn.
18. **Swim session.** If you have pool access, a summer favourite.

## Workout lifestyle (19-25)

19. **Meal prep Sunday.** What you eat in a week, with rough macros.
20. **Supplements and kit review.** What you use, what you do not, and why.
21. **Rest day.** Recovery, a walk, a bath, honest about the days off.
22. **Gym bag tour.** Everything you carry.
23. **Q&A.** Training questions answered on video.
24. **Weekly check-in.** Weight, measurements or how you feel, only what you are comfortable sharing.
25. **Workout plan PPV.** A four-week plan as a PDF or a video series.

## Safety and honesty

- **Do not give medical advice.** Say "this is what works for me" and suggest fans check with a professional before starting a new programme.
- **Do not overpromise results.** "Lose 10 kg in a month" sets fans up to fail and invites complaints.
- **Film safely.** No phone balanced on a bar, no max lifts without a spotter, no unsafe outdoor spots for a better angle.
- **Respect the gym.** Many gyms have filming policies. Ask before you film, and keep other members out of shot.
- **Mind your recovery.** A content schedule should not push you into training injured.

## How to film fitness well

- **Tripod at hip height,** side-on for most lifts. Front-on for squats and presses.
- **Shoot horizontal for full sessions, vertical for clips.**
- **Natural light or a single bright light.** Gym lighting is usually overhead and harsh; find the best spot and use it every time.
- **Mic the voiceover separately** if you narrate, because gyms are loud.
- **Pull stills from 4K video** so one session gives you photos and clips.
