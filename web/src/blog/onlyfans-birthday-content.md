Birthdays give you two content moments a year per relationship: yours, and each fan's. Your birthday is a natural reason to celebrate with your audience. A fan's birthday is a chance to make one person feel remembered. Here are nineteen ideas across both.

## Remembering fan birthdays

Before the ideas, the system. You cannot celebrate a birthday you forgot.

- When a fan mentions their birthday, write it down that minute.
- Keep it in one place per fan, not in a chatter's head or a sticky note.
- Check the list every Monday for the week ahead.

If you run Inflowwww, the fan panel in Messages Pro has a nickname, free-form notes and tags per fan, shared across your team. A note like "birthday 14 March" and a tag like "birthday-march" is enough to build a monthly list. There is no dedicated birthday field; notes and tags do the job.

## Your birthday: photo and video sets (1-7)

1. **Cake smash.** Messy, fun, colourful. Shoot on a washable floor.
2. **Confetti set.** Metallic confetti and a fast shutter speed or burst mode.
3. **Balloons.** Number balloons for your age, or a balloon arch as a backdrop.
4. **Dressed up, dressed down.** The party outfit, then the after-party loungewear.
5. **Cake tasting.** A slow, playful clip of the first slice.
6. **Prop set.** Party hats, streamers, a sash, a crown.
7. **The wish.** Candles lit, eyes closed, a cut before the reveal.

## Your birthday: interactive and tip ideas (8-13)

8. **Post your wishlist.** Use a wishlist service that hides your address. Fans love a concrete way to give.
9. **Birthday wish post.** Tell fans one thing you want to do this year.
10. **"Reasons to tip me on my birthday."** A playful list post with a tip goal.
11. **Age-themed caption series.** "One photo for every year", if you have the patience.
12. **Birthday dance.** A short clip to your favourite song.
13. **Party invitation.** A live stream "party" with a time, a theme and a dress code.

Keep the asks light. A birthday works because it is a celebration, not a sales event.

## Your birthday: the live and the bundle (14-16)

14. **Birthday live.** Open with a toast, run a Q&A, end with a thank-you.
15. **Party prop poses.** Use leftover props for a week of extra posts.
16. **Birthday bundle.** Everything from your birthday week, priced as one PPV, for 72 hours.

## Fans' birthdays (17-19)

17. **A personal message.** First name or nickname, one line about something they told you, and a thank-you. Two sentences is enough.
18. **A small gift.** A free photo, a short voice note or a discount on their next PPV.
19. **A birthday wall.** With permission, a monthly post thanking fans with birthdays that month, by nickname.

Do not send fan-birthday messages from a template that looks like one. If you use a script, change a line every time. If you run Inflowwww, Scripts (`/growth/scripts`) holds reusable message templates, and Smart Messages (`/growth/smart-messages`) can schedule a send to a list.

## A birthday-week plan

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 7 days before | Announce your birthday week; share the wishlist |
| 3 days before | Teaser from the cake or confetti shoot |
| The day | Full set, wish post, live in the evening |
| Day after | Thank-you post; launch the 72-hour bundle |
| 3 days after | Last call on the bundle; thank the buyers |

For fan birthdays, the plan is simpler: check the list Monday, send the message on the day, log what you sent in the notes.
