September is a reset month. Summer ends, routines return, and fans settle back into habits, including the habit of checking your page. Here are thirty ideas built on that shift, with a plan to shoot them in four sessions.

## The dates worth using

- **First Monday:** Labor Day in the US and Canada.
- **Early September:** back to work and back to routines.
- **September 19:** Talk Like a Pirate Day, if your audience likes a silly one.
- **September 21:** World Gratitude Day.
- **Around September 22-23:** the autumn equinox, the first day of autumn in the northern hemisphere.
- **End of month:** payday for many fans.

## Week 1 (1-7): ease into the season

1. **Spa day.** Face mask, robe, candles.
2. **Labor Day lounging.** A lazy holiday set.
3. **Book club.** Glasses, a cardigan, a favourite novel.
4. **Midweek motivation.** A goal for the month, and a question to fans about theirs.
5. **Throwback.** An early set next to a recent one.
6. **Fitness Friday.** A short workout clip.
7. **Sleepy Saturday.** Morning light, messy hair, coffee.

## Week 2 (8-14): routines

8. **Soft Sunday.** Calm, slow, low effort.
9. **Voice day.** A voice note or ASMR clip instead of a photo.
10. **Gear Tuesday.** Review something you use for content.
11. **Domestic day.** Cooking, cleaning, a home-life set.
12. **Thankful Thursday.** Thank the fans who have been around longest.
13. **Niche Friday.** Lean into your page's specific angle.
14. **Self-care Saturday.** Your real routine.

## Week 3 (15-21): interactive

15. **Ratings day.** Fans send something to rate: outfits, pets, setups.
16. **Cosplay Monday.** A character fans voted for.
17. **Teaser Tuesday.** A crop of an upcoming PPV.
18. **Advice day.** Answer fans' questions about dating, routines or anything you are comfortable with.
19. **Pirate day.** A playful costume, if it fits your brand.
20. **Fan Friday.** Thank top supporters by nickname, with permission.
21. **World Gratitude Day.** A sincere post about what you are grateful for this year.

## Week 4 and the tail (22-30): autumn arrives

22. **First day of autumn.** Knitwear, leaves, warm light.
23. **Never have I ever.** A game post with ten statements.
24. **Trivia Tuesday.** A quiz about you, with a prize for a perfect score.
25. **Q&A.** Answer the best questions from the month.
26. **Story time.** A real story, told to camera.
27. **Payday PPV.** Release your best set of the month when wallets are full.
28. **Soundtrack Saturday.** Share your playlist; film a dance.
29. **Autumn niche set.** Your usual niche, styled for the season.
30. **Recap.** Best moments of September; tease October and Halloween.

## Shooting it in four sessions

- **Session 1 (late August):** cosy and home sets (1, 2, 7, 8, 11, 14).
- **Session 2:** themed looks (3, 16, 19, 22, 29).
- **Session 3:** fitness and outdoor (6, plus extra frames).
- **Session 4 (mid-September):** the payday PPV and teasers (17, 27).

The talking days (motivation, advice, Q&A, story, recap) take ten minutes each. Film on the day.

Back-to-work lists usually include a laptop, a bag and a software licence. Ours is MIT, and it costs nothing to renew.

### Don't skip planning October

September is when you plan Halloween. Pick three costumes and run a vote in the last week, so the shoot can happen in early October. See our October and Halloween posts for the full plan.
