## How the For You feed decides

TikTok's For You feed is personalised for each viewer. TikTok describes the main signals as how people interact with videos (watching to the end, rewatching, sharing, commenting, following), details of the video (sounds, hashtags, captions), and device and account settings.

For you as a creator, that means:

- **Follower count matters less than on other apps.** A new account can reach many people with one good video.
- **Watch time is the core signal.** A video people watch to the end beats a video with a great thumbnail and a slow middle.
- **Consistency trains your audience.** Posting in one recognisable lane helps TikTok find the people who like it.

## Pick a lane you can sustain

Choose one or two themes that you would enjoy making videos about for a year:

- **A skill:** makeup, cooking, fitness, styling, photography, editing.
- **A personality format:** storytimes, day-in-the-life, answering questions, commentary on trends.
- **A hobby community:** gaming, books, alt fashion, cosplay (in costume, within the guidelines), plants, pets.

Then make your profile match: a clear photo, a username that is the same on every platform, and a bio that says what your videos are about. No adult references, no adult links.

## Use trends without losing yourself

Trends are TikTok's shared language. They make your video easier to understand and easier to recommend.

1. **Spend ten minutes a day in your own For You feed** and save trends that fit your lane.
2. **Use trending sounds** from TikTok's library while they are rising, not after everyone has done them.
3. **Add your own twist.** The trend gets you shown; your angle gets you followed.
4. **Skip trends that don't fit you.** A mismatched trend brings views but not the right followers.

Use a few hashtags that describe the video and your niche. Skip generic tags that say nothing about the content.

## Make videos people finish

- **Hook in the first second:** a question, a bold line of text, or the most interesting moment first.
- **Cut every pause.** Edit in TikTok's editor or a separate app, and keep it tight.
- **Add on-screen text** so it works without sound.
- **End with a reason to follow:** "Part two tomorrow" or a question for the comments.
- **Light your face well.** A window or a ring light is enough.

## Build a community, not just views

Views fade; communities stay.

- **Reply to comments,** and reply with videos to good questions. It creates your next idea and shows viewers you are listening.
- **Go Live** once you are eligible, if it suits you. Lives build a closer connection than any single video.
- **Collaborate** with creators in your lane through duets and stitches.
- **Post on a schedule** you can keep. Once a day is a strong target; three or four times a week is fine if it is consistent.
- **Check TikTok analytics weekly** for watch time, traffic sources and follower activity times, and post when your followers are online.
