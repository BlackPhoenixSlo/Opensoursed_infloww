Short answer: X is the main promotion channel for most creators because it allows adult content when marked as sensitive and lets you link to OnlyFans directly. What works there is a profile that converts, previews that tease rather than give away, a steady rhythm, and real engagement. Here are eleven specific tips.

First, read X's rules on adult content and sensitive media and set your media settings to match. Everything below assumes you follow them.

## Your profile (tips 1 to 3)

**1. Keep your profile photos current.** Your avatar shows next to every post, so make it sharp, well lit and recognisable. Update the header every month or two with recent work, and check how it crops on phones.

**2. Write a short bio with one call to action.** X gives you 160 characters. Spend them on who you are, what subscribers get, and one instruction: "Daily posts, I reply to every message. Subscribe below." Two or three emoji at most. Update it when you run an offer.

**3. Link directly, and track it.** X lets you put your OnlyFans link in your profile and posts, so skip the link page there. Use a tracking link rather than your bare URL so you can see how many subscribers X sends. If you run Inflowwww, make one in **Tracking links** (`/growth/tracking-links`).

## Your content (tips 4 to 6)

**4. Make X-specific previews.** Don't post the same full content subscribers paid for. Subscribers notice, and it removes the reason to subscribe. Post previews, alternate angles and outtakes instead.

**5. Tease with crops and covers.** A cropped or covered version of a set makes people curious about the rest. Always mark adult media as sensitive.

**6. Keep quality high and editing light.** Good light and a steady camera matter more than expensive gear. Edit lightly; heavy filters make people trust you less, and candid shots often outperform polished ones.

## Your reach (tips 7 to 9)

**7. Engage with bigger accounts in your niche.** Reply thoughtfully to creators and accounts your audience already follows. Repost what you genuinely like. Don't repost or reply in bulk; it reads as spam.

**8. Build reciprocal partnerships.** Repost trades and shout-out swaps with creators your size are one of the fastest ways to grow. Agree on days, wording and timing. Ask permission before tagging repost accounts, and treat paid reposts as advertising.

**9. Post on a schedule your audience can learn.** Find when your followers are online from X's analytics or a two-week test of different time slots, then post at those times every day. Our post on building an X posting schedule has a template.

## Your conversion (tips 10 and 11)

**10. Give people a reason to subscribe today.** A free trial link with an end date, a discount for a birthday week, or a new series launching tonight. Say what subscribers get: how often you post, what kind of content, whether you reply. If you run Inflowwww, make named trial links in **Free trial links** (`/growth/free-trial-links`) so each campaign is counted separately.

**11. Watch your costs as closely as your clicks.** Every tool you add to your promotion stack (schedulers, link tools, paid reposts, management software) is a monthly line on your statement. Total them once a quarter and drop anything that does not clearly pay for itself.

## Putting it together

A simple weekly loop that uses all eleven:

- **Monday:** update the bio offer and pinned post. Plan the week's previews.
- **Daily:** post at your best times, spend 15 minutes engaging after each post, reply to every comment.
- **Twice a week:** a repost trade or shout-out swap.
- **Sunday:** compare impressions, link clicks and subscribers per tracking link. Keep what converts.
