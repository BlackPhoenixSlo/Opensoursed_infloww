Most rejections come down to a document problem, a mismatch between your ID and your payout details, an unfinished profile or a rule you did not know about. Each one has a straightforward fix, and none of them needs a workaround.

## The usual reasons

OnlyFans does not always say exactly why an application failed, but the same causes come up again and again.

- **ID photo problems.** Glare, blur, cropped corners, an expired document or a photo of a screen instead of the physical card.
- **Selfie problems.** A selfie that does not clearly match the ID: poor light, a hat or glasses, a face partly out of frame.
- **Age.** Creators must be 18 or older. There is no fix for this one other than waiting.
- **Name mismatch.** The legal name on your ID, your account details and your bank or payout account must agree.
- **Incomplete profile.** Missing profile picture, banner, bio or payout details.
- **Policy issues.** Content or behaviour that breaks the Terms of Service or the Acceptable Use Policy.
- **Duplicate accounts.** Several applications from the same person or device can look suspicious.

## A fix-it checklist before you resubmit

Work through this list before trying again. Resubmitting the same thing usually gets the same answer.

1. Use a valid, unexpired government ID. Photograph the physical document on a dark, plain surface in daylight, with all four corners visible and no glare.
2. Take the selfie facing a window or another soft light source. Remove hats and sunglasses. Hold the camera at eye level.
3. Check that your legal name is spelled the same way everywhere: ID, account details, bank account.
4. Fill in every profile field: picture, banner, bio and payout information.
5. Read the Terms of Service and the Acceptable Use Policy, and remove anything from your profile that conflicts with them.
6. Use one account. If you already created extras by mistake, say so when you contact support rather than opening another.

## How to ask for a review

If you have fixed everything you can and the application is still rejected, contact OnlyFans support through the Help Center on the site. Keep the message short and factual:

- Your username and the email address on the account.
- When you applied and when you were rejected.
- What you changed since the last attempt.

Then wait for a reply. Opening a new account while a review is pending usually makes the situation harder to resolve.

## What not to do

Some sites and forum posts sell "verification services" or claim to know a shortcut. Do not use them. Using someone else's identity, or a document that is not yours, breaks the platform's rules and can put your earnings and your personal information at risk. The only reliable route is the official process with your own documents.

## Set yourself up for the next step

Once you are approved, the work starts: a bio that converts, a welcome message, a content library and a promotion plan. Treat the application as practice for that. The same habits, like checking details twice and reading the rules first, carry through.
