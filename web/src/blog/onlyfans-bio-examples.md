A good OnlyFans bio tells a visitor who you are, what they get and what to do next, and it does it in the first few lines. Here is a structure, four types to choose from, and examples you can adapt.

## Structure first

Most bios that convert follow the same order:

1. **Hook:** one line that gives the visitor a reason to keep reading.
2. **What you post:** the kind of content, in plain words.
3. **How often:** a posting rhythm fans can count on.
4. **How to interact:** whether you read messages, take requests or do customs.
5. **Call to action:** what the visitor should do now.

Short bios work, and longer ones work too, as long as they are easy to scan. Use line breaks and short sentences. Nobody reads a wall of text on a phone.

## Length and formatting

The bio field allows roughly 1,000 characters. You do not need all of them. Only the first lines show before a visitor taps to expand, so put the hook and the core offer there.

- Aim for 300 to 600 characters for most pages.
- Use line breaks between ideas.
- A few symbols as bullet points can help scanning. Use them sparingly so the bio still reads cleanly.
- Read it on a phone before you save it.

## Four types that work

Pick the one that fits your persona.

- **The teaser.** Short and curious. Example: "New here? Start with the pinned post. You will know in ten seconds if this is your page."
- **The story.** Personal and warm. Example: "Bookworm by day, photographer by night. This page is the part of my life I actually choose to share."
- **The menu.** Bullet points listing exactly what fans get. Example: "Three new sets a week. Behind-the-scenes every Sunday. Messages answered daily."
- **The niche.** Leads with what makes you different. Example: "Cosplay, every costume built by hand. Full process posts for subscribers."

## What to include and what to leave out

Include:

- Your posting schedule.
- What is on the feed and what comes as paid messages, so nobody feels misled.
- Whether you take custom requests, and how to ask.
- A hint of personality: a hobby, a running joke, a recurring series.

Leave out:

- Prices that will change soon, unless you update the bio when they do.
- Promises you will not keep, such as "I reply within an hour" if you will not.
- Anything that identifies your real location or employer.
- Links to other subscription platforms. OnlyFans' rules limit what you can promote on your page; read them before you add any link.

## End with a call to action

Close with one clear instruction. "Subscribe and say hi; I read every first message." "Check the pinned post for this week's schedule." One action is better than three.

Then test. Change one line at a time, leave it for two weeks, and compare how many profile visitors became subscribers. Create a tracking link for each promotion source in your OnlyFans settings, so you can see which sources convert best with each version.
