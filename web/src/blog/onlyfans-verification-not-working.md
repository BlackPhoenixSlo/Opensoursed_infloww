OnlyFans verification has four steps: confirm your email, complete your profile, verify your ID with a live selfie, and add payout details in your legal name. When it fails, the cause is almost always a photo, a name mismatch or a missing field, and each can be fixed.

## The four steps

The exact screens change from time to time, so follow the prompts on the site. The order is roughly:

1. **Verify your email address.** Click the link in the confirmation email. Check spam if it does not arrive.
2. **Complete your profile.** Add a profile picture, banner, display name and bio.
3. **Verify your ID with a live selfie.** Use a phone with a camera to photograph a valid government ID, then take the live selfie the platform compares to your ID photo. You must be 18 or older.
4. **Add payout details.** Enter your bank or payout information in your own legal name.

Then wait for review. Approval often comes within a few days.

## Why verification fails

The most common causes:

- **Unreadable ID photo:** blur, glare, low light, a finger over the text, cropped corners.
- **Expired or unsupported document.**
- **Selfie does not match:** different lighting, a hat or glasses, the face partly out of frame, or a large change in appearance since the ID photo.
- **Name mismatch** between the ID, the account details and the payout account.
- **Incomplete profile:** a missing picture, bio or payout field.
- **Technical problems:** camera permission denied in the browser, an outdated browser, a weak connection that drops the upload.
- **Age:** applicants under 18 cannot be verified.

## Troubleshooting checklist

Work through this before you try again.

1. Use a valid, unexpired passport, national ID card or driving licence.
2. Photograph the physical document, not a copy or a screen, on a plain dark surface in daylight. All four corners visible, no glare.
3. For the selfie, face a window or soft light, remove hats and sunglasses, and hold the phone at eye level.
4. Check that your legal name is spelled identically on the ID, the account and the payout account.
5. Fill in every profile field.
6. Update your browser, allow camera access for the site, and use a stable Wi-Fi connection.
7. If one device keeps failing, try another phone or browser.

## How to contact support

If you have fixed everything above and verification still fails, contact OnlyFans support through the Help Center on the site, which lists the current contact options. Include:

- Your username and the email address on the account.
- The date of each attempt and any error message, word for word.
- What you changed between attempts.

Be patient with the reply. Do not open a second account while you wait; duplicate accounts complicate reviews.

## What not to do

Do not use someone else's documents, edited images or services that offer to verify an account for you. They break the platform's rules and can put your earnings and identity at risk. The official process, with your own ID, is the only route that works long term.
