You can earn on OnlyFans without posting nudes. Plenty of creators run SFW or suggestive pages built on personality, a niche or a skill. Here is what sells in each category, and how to price it.

## Visual content that is not explicit

**Photos.** The categories that tend to work:

- **Lingerie, swimwear and fashion sets.** Styled, well lit, and themed.
- **Cosplay.** A character, a costume, a full set across several looks.
- **Niche close-ups.** Hands, feet, nails, tattoos, jewellery. Small audiences, but loyal ones.
- **Lifestyle.** Travel, gym, cooking, a day in your life.

**Videos.**

- **Try-on hauls** with a vote on the favourite.
- **Workouts and routines,** from a short warm-up to a full session.
- **Talking-to-camera:** stories, advice, reactions, Q&As.
- **ASMR and relaxation:** whispering, tapping, ambient sound.

Keep the style consistent so fans know what they are paying for. If the page is SFW, say so in the bio. It filters out fans expecting something else and cuts refund requests.

## Written content

**Scripts and stories.** Short fiction, roleplay scripts for fans to read along with, serialised stories released weekly. A weekly chapter is a reason to renew.

**Pay-per-view writing.** Guides you are qualified to write: "my 4-week beginner gym plan", "how I pack for a two-week trip in carry-on", "my skincare routine with products and prices". Package them as PPV or as a paid PDF.

A useful test: if a friend would pay for your advice on it, a stranger probably will too.

## Digital products

These sell once and keep selling.

- **Presets and filters** for photo apps, if your feed has a recognisable look.
- **Wallpapers and printable art.**
- **Workout or meal plans.**
- **Templates:** caption packs, posting calendars, budgeting sheets.

Price low ($5-$20) and bundle. A bundle of three presets at $15 usually sells better than one at $8.

## Merchandise and services

**Merch.** Use print-on-demand so you hold no stock and never ship from home. Start with one product (a sticker or a print) and add more only if it sells.

**Services.** Your time is the product:

- Personalised voice or video messages.
- Coaching in a skill: fitness, posing, makeup, content planning.
- Feedback sessions: fans send photos of their outfits or setups and you reply with notes.
- Gaming sessions or watch-alongs.

Set a price per minute or per item, write down what is and is not included, and stick to it. Boundaries are easier to hold when they are printed in the menu.

We would put our own price in the menu, but the column only has zeros.

## Pricing and positioning

- **Subscription:** keep it low enough to try, especially for SFW pages.
- **PPV:** price by effort. A 30-second clip and a 20-minute tutorial are not the same product.
- **Bundles:** offer a monthly "everything this month" bundle for the fans who want it all.
- **Free trial links** bring new fans in for a few days before they commit. If you run Inflowwww, Free trial links (`/growth/free-trial-links`) and Tracking links (`/growth/tracking-links`) sit under Growth, so you can see which promo channel actually converts.

## Start this week

1. Pick **one** category from above that you can produce weekly.
2. Write a bio line that says what the page is.
3. Post four times in the first week and run one poll.
4. Add a second category after a month, based on what got replies.
